Visual Discovery Space: Sampling Distribution

About This Activity

This activity demonstrates the Central Limit Theorem in action. Pick a parent population, draw repeated samples, and watch the distribution of sample means gradually take on a normal shape — no matter what the original population looked like.

How to Use This Lab

  1. Start with the defaults. Leave the distribution and sample size at their default settings, then click "Grab One Sample" to watch a single sample get drawn and its mean animate into the sampling distribution below, or click "Grab Batch" to quickly generate many sample means at once.
  2. Observe the pattern. Repeat the previous step and watch the sampling distribution (bottom histogram) take shape as sample means accumulate — notice its overall shape, center, and spread.
  3. Increase the sample size. Reset, then run the same distribution again at increasing sample sizes (e.g., n = 10, n = 15, then n = 50). Compare how quickly the sampling distribution takes on a bell shape at each size.
  4. Switch distributions. Change the parent distribution (try Uniform, Normal, Exponential, and Bimodal) and repeat the process. Compare how fast each distribution's sampling distribution converges to a bell shape.
